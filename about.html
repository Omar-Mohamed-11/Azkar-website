About the Project

Azkar is a simple, comfortable place to read daily Islamic Azkar. It brings the morning and evening remembrances together with a small Tasbeeh counter, with the focus kept on the words and the act of reading.

Features

  • Morning Azkar
  • Evening Azkar
  • Tasbeeh counter
  • Responsive design
  • Simple navigation
  • Comfortable reading experience

Technologies

HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.
